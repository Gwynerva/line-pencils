# Gallery

Every pencil type that is known to be realizable, for 1 to 13 lines, drawn from its exact certificate, plus three
arrangements of 14 lines. Choose the number of lines below; click a drawing to enlarge it, see the exact coefficients
of its lines, and point at an intersection to light up the lines through it.

Points on more lines are drawn larger. Within each drawing the shade also grows with the number of lines, from the
lightest to the darkest blue present; the enlarged view lists what each shade means.

Each drawing is a projective image of the certified coordinates, chosen so that all intersection points are visible
and well separated; the incidences are exactly those of the certificate. A field such as ℚ(√5) next to a type means
that the certificate uses that square root; the field is the one in which the example was found, and only for the
pentagram {5, 3<sup>10</sup>, 2<sup>5</sup>} is the irrationality known to be necessary. For n = 12 and 13 the list is a lower bound: a few
types are still undecided ([open problems](open.md)).
